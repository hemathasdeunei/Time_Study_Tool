Time Study Tool

Hemath AS (www.hemath.com) Productivity

A professional desktop application for industrial engineers and operations teams to conduct and manage time studies on the shop floor.

Description

Time Study Tool helps you organize your work by projects and operations. For each operation, record multiple timed observations across defined process steps — the app captures both continuous (cumulative) and elemental (step) times simultaneously. Run multiple versions of a time study on the same operation to track improvements over time.

Key Features
  1. 1Create and manage multiple projects with color-coded organization
  2. 2Define operations with department, shift, supervisor, and engineer details
  3. 3Build step lists and record timestamped observations with a single tap
  4. 4View recorded studies with continuous and elemental times side by side
  5. 5Export any time study to a formatted Excel "Time Observation Form" with freeze panes, color-coded values, and summary rows
  6. 6All data stored locally — no internet connection required, no accounts, no subscriptions
